How It Works

From Quote to Cured Floor

A clean, five-step process built around a real bond — not a coat of paint over cracked concrete.

1

Free Quote

Call, text, or fill out the form. TJ walks the space with you and gives you an honest, no-pressure price.

2

Grind & Prep

Every slab is mechanically diamond-ground to strip old coatings, oil, paint, and glue and open the concrete pores for a real bond.

3

Epoxy Base Coat

A 100% solids epoxy base coat goes down first, bonding deep into the slab instead of just sitting on top of it.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does an epoxy floor installation take?

Most residential garages are done in 1–2 days from grinding to final topcoat. Larger commercial spaces take longer depending on square footage — TJ will give you a firm timeline with your quote.

How long until I can park in my garage again?

You can typically walk on the floor within 24 hours and drive on it within a few days. TJ will give you exact cure times for your specific system when he quotes the job.

What’s the difference between flake and solid color epoxy?

Solid color epoxy is a clean, single-color finish that’s simple and budget-friendly. Flake epoxy broadcasts vinyl flake into the base coat for texture, extra grip, and better hiding of everyday dirt and tire marks.

Do you remove old paint, oil stains, or a previous coating first?

Yes. Every slab is mechanically diamond-ground before anything goes down, which strips old coatings, oil, paint, and glue and opens the concrete so the new epoxy actually bonds instead of peeling later.

What does the 15-year warranty cover?

The warranty covers adhesion and workmanship — if the coating doesn’t bond right or the install fails to hold up as it should, TJ stands behind it.

Do you serve areas outside Decatur?

Yes — Springfield, Champaign, Urbana, Savoy, Mattoon, and surrounding Central Illinois communities. If you’re not sure you’re in range, just call or text.
